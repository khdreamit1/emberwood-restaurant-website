The Space

A Setting Built for Occasions

Our banquet hall is designed to adapt — from an intimate rehearsal dinner to a full wedding reception or corporate gathering. Warm lighting, flexible seating, and a dedicated events team come together to make planning easy and the event itself effortless.

Every event includes coordination support, customizable menus, and access to our in-house catering team, so the details are handled from first inquiry to last toast.
