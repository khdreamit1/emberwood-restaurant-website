Where We Started

Built Around the Table

Emberwood began with a simple belief: a restaurant should feel like it was made for the people in it.

We're a modern American restaurant currently preparing to open our doors, built by people who have spent years in kitchens and dining rooms that valued craft over spectacle. Our menu draws on classic technique and seasonal, quality ingredients, served in a room designed to feel warm rather than formal.

Every detail — from the lighting to the pacing of a meal — is considered with one goal: to give guests an evening they'll want to return to.

What Guides Us

Our Philosophy

Food

Quality Ingredients

We build the menu around what's in season, sourced from growers and purveyors we trust, and prepared with restraint so the ingredients speak for themselves.

Service

Genuine Hospitality

Our team is trained to read the table — celebratory, quiet, quick — and shape the evening around it, not the other way around.

Craft

Considered Detail

From plating to lighting to the pacing between courses, small decisions are made deliberately, not by default.
